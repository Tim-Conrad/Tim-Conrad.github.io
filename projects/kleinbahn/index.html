Kleinbahn

An island-railway sandbox in three.js, built for phones.

Play it here

Play it here

This is the game, not a video. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap the plus button to build.

Open it full screen

The demo loads from tiny-train.beacon.host, and only after you press play.

Kleinbahn is a sandbox. There is no score and no goal. You shape islands, lay track and roads, and place things: stations, houses, a harbour, a lighthouse, a volcano. The game adds people, cars and boats around them.

It is made for a phone held in one hand. The game is one self-contained HTML file. An island can be shared as a link or a QR code.

The video shows the sample island that the game starts with.

  • Trains and trackSwitches, bridges, stations and trains that stop for each other.
  • The islandTowns, people, cars, level crossings, harbours and drawbridges.
  • EventsA volcano, a shuttle launch, an airship, hot-air balloons and a fire brigade.
  • Time and weatherDay and night, four seasons, rain and clouds. In autumn there is a ghost train ride.
  • Shared playOne island kept the same on several devices.

How it changed

One entry per merged pull request that changed play, newest first. Each clip is filmed from the game at the state of that pull request.

  1. 27 Nov 2026 · 4 changes

  2. PR #50

    Rules on high ground

    What the island had before now works on raised land too. A big base placed on a hill, like the airfield or a Zeppelin base, levels the tiles under it, and the toast says how many. The clip puts a Zeppelin base on the side of a mountain.

    • A driven car and the animals stop at a cliff.
    • The Zeppelin, the balloons and the planes keep clear of the mountains.
    • Lamps, skid marks, taps and the follow camera sit at the height of the ground.

  3. PR #48 and #49

    Mountains

    A new Hills tool under Nature raises and lowers the land and makes ramps, mountains and valleys. Between the levels the game draws cliffs, stairs and waterfalls, and high ground gets rock, scree and snow. The clip makes a mountain with the tool and turns round it.

    • Track, streets and people climb only by ramps.
    • Houses need flat ground. Harbours and the shore stay at sea level.
    • In shared play, a height change of the other player rebuilds the land.
    • PR #48 added the heights underneath. Share links and saves keep them.
    • PR #51 fixed shared play: the host draws the track and props of a guest again.

  4. PR #46

    One big map

    Every island now lives on one map of 144 × 120 tiles, four times the old 72 × 60. An old save or share link opens in the middle, with every tile in its place. The clip rises from the island in the middle until it sees the whole map.

    • The airfield of the sample island stands in a new place.
    • Shared play needs the same version on both devices: an older game cannot join.

  5. PR #38

    A longer Geisterbahn

    The ride through the Geisterbahn lasts 188 seconds instead of 81, with seven new rooms, among them the kitchen of the witch, the crypt of the vampire, a swamp and the throne hall of the Ghost King. On the way 37 little monsters show: three fast taps pop one, and the Ghost King takes 28. The clip taps the Ghost King in his hall until he falls.

    • At the end the ride shows the count and the best count.
    • On the island, twelve carts run, and guests and staff wear costumes.
    • The ride draws less: at most 125 draw calls a frame, 392 before.
  6. 16 Nov 2026 · 5 changes

  7. PR #35

    Finer track

    Seen from a driven train the track is close, so it got finer: the gravel bed and the rails run in one piece, curves are smooth, and the rails have a shiny head on a rusty foot. At a junction the beds merge into one and long timbers reach under both tracks. The clip turns slowly over a junction.

    • Plates under the rails show only close up, so the island far away costs no more.
    • A level crossing shows only the rails across the road.

  8. PR #33 and #34

    Drive the trains

    A train can be driven: the stick opens the regulator and brakes, and three track buttons set the next junction left, straight on or right, with a glowing arrow on the track. The clip drives a steam train, blows the whistle and sends it down a branch.

    • Stop beside a platform for a “Perfect stop” and passengers getting on and off.
    • Stopped, hold the stick back to change ends.
    • The train still stops by itself for a train ahead, a raised drawbridge and the buffers.
    • PR #36 fixed a blue screen when you took a train.

  9. PR #32

    Drift, horn and a painted sea

    Cars, boats and their drivers got special moves. A car drifts with skid marks and tyre smoke, boosts and honks, and people hop out of the way. The motor boat boosts, jumps off wave crests and can paint its wake in colours. The sailing boat sails by the wind and tacks. The clip drifts a car, then paints the sea from the motor boat.

    • A fast car flies over the hump of a level crossing.
    • The move buttons sit on the left, so the right thumb steers (PR #31).

  10. PR #27

    Stunts and smoke in the sky

    A plane you fly moves freely in three dimensions: it can loop, roll and fly upside down, slows in a climb and speeds up in a dive. New buttons give a boost, a barrel roll, a loop and a turn-back, and the smoke comes in colours that stay in the sky, so you can draw with it. The clip boosts, rolls and loops in red smoke, then turns back in rainbow smoke.

    • Holding the stick up flies a loop as part of the flight; PR #29 made that loop wider.
    • Near the ground, the ceiling and the edge of the map, the plane turns away by itself.
    • Planes on their tours fly their loops the same way.
    • In shared play the others see your smoke colour and your boost.

  11. PR #25

    Five kinds of volcano

    A volcano can be a stratovolcano, a shield volcano, a cinder cone, a lava dome or a caldera, and each kind erupts in its own way. The clip shows one volcano as each kind in turn, then an eruption of the cinder cone.

    • Pick the kind when you place a volcano, or later in its panel.
    • A volcano erupts only when you start it.
    • The camera no longer shakes.
    • Eruptions cost less time per frame.
  12. 10 Nov 2026 · 5 changes

  13. PR #16

    Finer people, cars and boats

    Near the camera, cars, vans, trucks, bicycles and people get detailed models: wheels, windows and lights on the cars, a frame, a saddle and cranks on the bicycles. The clip rides behind a car through town.

    • Boats have proper hulls, and each boat type more detail.
    • Visiting boats look for a free berth.
    • Cars fit their lane and steer when they change lanes.
    • Cyclists sit on the saddle with their feet on the pedals.

  14. PR #15

    A loading screen

    While the game loads, a picture of the town shows with the Kleinbahn logo. The train in the logo runs off the rail and comes back. The clip shows the screen, then the game behind it.

    • A home-screen icon: the red steam engine of the logo.
    • With reduced motion, the logo train stands still.

  15. PR #14

    Every player steers

    In shared play every player can steer a plane, the airship, a car or a boat. In the clip a second player, Mia, flies a plane, and the host follows her.

    • A plane or the airship has one pilot at a time.
    • A chip for each player shows what the player does. A tap follows the player.
    • An arrow at the screen edge points to each player out of sight.

  16. PR #13

    An airfield

    A runway, hangars, a tower and five planes that take off, fly a route with a loop and land in turn. The clip shows one of them in its loop.

    • One plane tows a banner.
    • Pilot mode: fly a plane or the airship with one stick.
    • Drive a car, a bicycle or a boat yourself.
    • Cars drive only to places their road network reaches.

  17. PR #11

    Fires in shared play

    The host starts a fire, and every guest sees the same house burn and the fire brigade drive out.

    • Island links and saves are checked before they load.
    • An island link asks before it replaces your island.
    • The QR code is drawn offline.
  18. 8 Nov 2026 · 6 changes

  19. PR #9

    New icons

    All 60 icons were drawn again, centred and sized for phones. The clip opens the build dial.

    • Each dial item shows its name under the icon.
    • Larger touch targets.
    • The dial no longer runs past the screen edge.

  20. PR #8

    Rooms that survive a reload

    When the host stops, every guest goes home at once. In the clip the guest is on the host’s island without trees, the host closes the room, and the guest’s own island comes back.

    • A host that reloads opens the same room again.
    • A guest that loses the host waits and joins again by itself.

  21. PR #7

    Player names

    A player can give a name. It shows in the player list and in the follow bar: here the host follows Mia.

  22. PR #3

    See the other players

    A pin in each player’s colour stands where that player looks. In the clip the pin of a guest moves across the island.

    • Follow another player’s view.
    • The host can make a guest view only, or send it away.

  23. PR #2

    The host decides when things move

    Eruptions, launches and flights start on the host and reach every guest. Here the game is a guest: the host starts an eruption, and the guest’s volcano erupts with the same lava.

    • Airships and balloons keep the host’s position.
    • Trains keep their place on the host’s path.

  24. PR #1

    Shared play

    One player hosts and friends join by link or QR code. In the clip a second player plants trees, and they appear on the host’s island.

    • Remote edits go into the undo history, so an undo takes back only your own change.
    • A guest never saves. Its own island comes back when it leaves.
    • Every message from another player is checked.
