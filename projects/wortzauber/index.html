Wortzauber

A letter-tracing word game for children, in German and English.

Play it here

This is the game, not a video. It is made for a phone held sideways, so it is easier to play full screen.

Play it here
Open it full screen

The demo loads from wortzauber.beacon.host, and only after you press play.

A child says, types or handwrites a word, then traces each letter along school lines. When the last stroke is done, the thing appears in the game and can be picked up and used.

The game has a story. A dragon has taken the Queen’s spell book. The hero helps Berta the baker make a cake, goes through a dark mouse hole with a candle, and collects letters in the castle. A small hovering robot gives hints.

It is plain HTML and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies. The video shows the German version.

  • Write, trace, appearEvery word is traced letter by letter. The game shows each stroke first and helps when a child is stuck.
  • Three ways to writeBy voice, on a child-sized keyboard, or freely by hand.
  • PlacesMeadow, village street, bakery, mouse hole and castle, joined by a map.
  • SumsMaths riddles with apples and candles.
  • Play togetherTwo children can play the same game on two devices.
