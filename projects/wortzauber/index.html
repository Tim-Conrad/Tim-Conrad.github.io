Wortzauber

A letter-tracing word game for children. Write a word, trace every letter, and it appears in the world.

Try the real thing

This is the game itself, not a video. It is made for a phone held sideways, so it plays best full screen.

Play it here
Open it full screen

The demo loads from wortzauber.beacon.host, and only after you press play.

In Wortzauber, words are magic. A child says, types or handwrites a word, then traces each letter along the school lines. When the last stroke is done, the thing they wrote appears, ready to be picked up and used.

There is a story to follow. The dragon has taken the Queen’s spell book, so the hero helps Berta the baker make her a cake, climbs through a dark mouse hole with a candle, and collects letters across the castle. A small hovering robot shows the way.

It is plain HTML and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies. The reel shows the game as it runs, in German.

  • Write, trace, appearEvery word is traced letter by letter, with a demo stroke and gentle help.
  • Three ways to writeBy voice, on a child-sized keyboard, or freely by hand.
  • A story worldMeadow, village street, bakery, mouse hole and castle, joined by a map.
  • Little sumsMaths riddles with apples and candles along the way.
  • Play togetherTwo children can share one adventure on two devices.
